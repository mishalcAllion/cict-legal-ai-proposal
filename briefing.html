In one minute

  • The productOne AI assistant platform for CICT's legal, compliance and risk, policy, board and records teams. It drafts, checks, translates and finds. Authorised CICT staff confirm every opinion, risk finding, policy and approval.
  • Two phasesPhase 1 is the shared platform plus contract management, records management and common support, and must work on its own. Phase 2 adds legal affairs, policy management, compliance and risk control, and board affairs. One year of 24/7 support sits inside the price.
  • The hard partThe platform more than the screens: data classification and masking, a gateway over several AI models, Chinese, English and Sinhala throughout, answers that cite the exact clause or page, and strict rules on where data may go.
  • The main riskUser numbers, response times, uptime and recovery targets are all left "to be defined", yet they become the acceptance bar. We would be fixing a price before the target is fixed.
  • Our directionQuote both phases at fixed prices. Phase 1 opens with a Discovery milestone that pins down the open numbers. The Phase 2 price holds on stated assumptions, and material changes go through change control.

Data handling · §1.2 to §2.4

How a request moves through the system

Our reading of the specification as one path, from a user's question to a confirmed result. The highlighted steps are the ones that make this more than a chatbot.

  1. Ask or upload

    Question, PDF, Word or Excel file, or imported business data. Scans go through OCR the user can correct. Unreadable parts are flagged, never skipped. §1.2

  2. Classify

    The uploader labels the category and a business owner confirms it. The system never downgrades a classification on its own. §1.3

  3. Find and mask

    Names, prices, accounts, contract numbers and more, across text, tables, attachments, comments, tracked changes and file properties. Consistent stand-in IDs; preview and correct. §1.4

  4. Approve release

    Anything leaving the approved boundary needs CICT's approval. Masking alone is not permission to send. §1.4.7

  5. Retrieve

    Search the versioned knowledge base in three languages, filtered by what this user may see, so answers never cite restricted material. §2.2

  6. Analyse

    An approved model, chosen per business scenario, through one gateway. If it's down, switch to another approved model or tell the user. §2.4

  7. Answer with sources

    Document, clause or page for every point. Facts, judgements and recommendations kept apart. Warnings when evidence is thin, conflicting or out of date. §2.3

  8. Review and confirm

    Output stays a draft until authorised CICT staff edit, review and approve it. Confirmed results go to the records archive, and the requester is notified. Intro §3.6

Four data categories set what each step may do

Public

Published laws, public standards, public company information

May sit in a public knowledge base, updated through approved channels.

Internal

Internal policies, routine registers, working documents

Company-approved cloud, access by department and position.

Sensitive

Contract prices, customer and payment data, personal data, unpublished board papers

Controlled environment, restricted access and export; masked and reviewed before wider use.

Confidential

Materials classed strictly confidential, major case files

Only where the environment meets the confidentiality rules. Otherwise no upload at all.

CICT says these categories are a starting point and it will confirm the final rules §1.3. The confidential tier may push us toward self-hosted models.

Platform · Ch 1–2, §3.7

Rules that apply to every module

Three languages §2.1 §2.2
Interface switches between Chinese, English and Sinhala. Full-text and semantic search work in all three.
Permissions everywhere §1.5 §3.7.1
By role, department, data category and task, covering files, search, answers, reports and exports. No one can make themselves an administrator.
Model choice §2.4
Connect, switch and upgrade models without vendor lock-in. Named examples: DeepSeek, Kimi, GPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok. Usage and cost visible per scenario.
Data stays inside §1.5
Files, indexes, prompts, model inputs and outputs, logs and backups stay in the approved cloud. No training use without approval.
Honest answers §2.3
Never invent laws, cases, clauses or data. Treat instructions hidden inside uploaded files as content, not commands.
Models earn their place §2.3
Each model is validated on CICT's own samples and goes live only after meeting the agreed accuracy, language and speed targets.
Running it §2.6 §3.7.4
Red, amber and green health dashboard with alerts. Upgrades with rollback. Admin console. Audit logs of logins, access, model calls, exports and permission changes.
Integrations §2.6
API documentation plus working links to office systems, ERP, LAMS and others within confirmed scope. Each link is defined before build.

Delivery and acceptance · §3.8.2

How CICT will accept it

CICT runs acceptance with confirmed business samples and test conditions. It covers at least these eight areas:

  1. 1Business functionsEvery operation, output, citation and review step in the specification.
  2. 2Translation quality100 random sentences, Chinese into English and Sinhala, scored by hand.
  3. 3Masking and data boundariesDetection, previews, manual fixes, approval-based release, no unauthorised sends.
  4. 4User permissionsNobody reaches material or results outside their scope.
  5. 5SecurityData leakage, prompt injection and prohibited outputs, against an agreed checklist.
  6. 6Knowledge and analysisRight law and policy versions, accurate citations, warnings when material is thin or conflicting.
  7. 7Performance and operationsContinuous running, task recovery, backup restore, upgrades and rollback, against agreed metrics.
  8. 8IntegrationReal data transfer, permissions, sync and error handling. API documents alone don't pass.

The translation test is the sharpest single bar

Minimum score of 4 out of 5 on Chinese to English and Chinese to Sinhala. The specification leaves sampling, scoring method and pass criteria to the acceptance plan, and says other translation directions are tested separately. Legal Sinhala is a weak spot for current models.

What CICT receives

  • Working system, admin console, model integration and an initial knowledge base
  • Source code, deployment and configuration documents
  • API documentation
  • Licences and cost breakdowns for models, cloud and third-party services
  • Deployment and data flow diagrams, and a list of external connections

Bid shape

Our direction

  1. Quote both phases, each at a fixed price, plus the year of operation and maintenance.
  2. Make Discovery the first Phase 1 milestone: confirm metrics, cloud, models, checklists and sample sets.
  3. Hold the Phase 2 price on the stated assumptions, as confirmed in Discovery A20.
  4. Send material changes through change control.
  5. Pricing figures sit with Rushanthi and Asiri.
